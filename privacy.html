Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy describes how DDevWorks handles information related to its mobile apps, including Num Easy (the “App”).

Information collected

As designed, Num Easy does not require an account or details such as your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or files. DDevWorks does not collect or store users’ personal information on its own servers through the App.

How information is used

Data entered for calculations is processed on your device to provide the App’s features. DDevWorks does not sell or share that data with third parties.

Third-party services

The App is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information related to downloads and app usage under its own policies. You can read them here:

Children’s privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children under 13. If you have concerns or believe a child’s personal information has been provided, contact the developer.

Security

Information processed locally by the App remains on the device, according to the behavior described above. We recommend keeping your device updated and protected.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the App or its practices change. The current version and its update date will be published on this page.
